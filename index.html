A QUICK BRIEFING

Words are your defense.

Simple controls. A wonderfully busy sky.

shield➤
shield ENTER ↵
  1. Read the incoming words. Each word belongs to a threat. Matching letters light up as you type.
  2. Type the whole word and press Enter. A correct word immediately neutralizes the threat, then your interceptor puts on the show. Uppercase and lowercase both work.
  3. Beat the blue boundary. Each threat that crosses the shield costs one heart. You begin with five.
  4. Survive the countdown. Earn points, complete the mission, and unlock the next challenge.

A few things to know

ENTER submits and clears your input. A wrong word launches nothing. Try again immediately.

BACKSPACE fixes a typo. ESC pauses. Switching tabs also pauses the game.

12 in a row = +1 heart. Build up to eight hearts. A wrong submission or a shield breach breaks the streak.

More speed, more points. Fast interceptions and streaks add bonuses. Finish with perfect submission accuracy for an extra reward.

WPM = correctly intercepted characters ÷ 5 ÷ gameplay minutes. Live WPM uses the last 15 seconds; average includes idle gameplay time. Pauses are excluded. Accuracy compares accepted characters with all submitted characters.
