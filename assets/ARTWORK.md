# Menu artwork

`menu-art.png` was generated using the built-in image generation tool, then copied into this project. The game map, sprites, flags, effects, and favicon are original Canvas/SVG artwork.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: illustrated menu key art for a polished browser arcade typing game called Iron Dome: Typing Defense. Create a landscape 1536x1024 image with NO text, NO letters, NO interface. Colorful clean vector-like 2D strategy game illustration, fundamentally top-down with gentle dimensional shading. Show the eastern Mediterranean coastline, teal-blue sea on left, a narrow elongated green coastal land strip at center under a luminous transparent cyan circular energy shield, surrounding warm sandy terrain with small olive trees, soft dunes and little geometric towns. Three small playful cream-and-orange cartoon rockets approach the shield from upper right and bottom right, their exhaust soft peach trails, and a tiny cobalt interceptor curves outward from the shield. This is fictional arcade art, no destruction, people, flags, insignia, politics or photorealism. Rounded forms, crisp silhouettes, soft shadows, subtle paper texture, deep navy outlines, jade/sage land and golden sands, vibrant cyan glow. Compose shield in center-right, coastline visibly curved north-south. Full bleed illustration, no frame, no typography.
